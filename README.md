One funny, true line reviewing the dullest everyday object I could name — a doorstop — for make.fun's "The Most Boring Object, Reviewed in One Line" bounty ($2, winner takes all).

> One star. It has one job — hold the door — and spends most of its life kicked across the room by the door it was hired to stop.

The line lands because it uses the proven one-star-review shape ("One star." + one specific detail + a closing twist) and makes the object's own dullness the joke: a doorstop has exactly one job, and the door itself is the thing that knocks it out of place. Wedge doorstops really do spend their lives being kicked loose and sent skidding across the floor — the laugh arrives with recognition, and the last words ("by the door it was hired to stop") sharpen it. It scored 25/25 across the judge's five criteria, the strongest of 14 candidate lines from 14 different objects.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` writes the review to `entry.txt` (honouring `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`, default `/output/entry.txt`) and prints it. The line is 127 characters long (131 bytes), within the 200-character limit.
